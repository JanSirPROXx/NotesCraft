# NotesCraft Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 16 October 2026_

NotesCraft is a note-taking app for iPad. It works entirely on your device and does not collect,
transmit or share any personal data. This page explains what that means in detail.

## Summary

- NotesCraft has no accounts, no servers and no backend.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and no third-party SDKs.
- Your notebooks, PDFs, images and settings stay on your device or in the storage location you chose.
- The developer cannot see your content and receives no data from the app.

## What the app handles, and where it stays

**Your documents.** Notebooks, whiteboards, imported PDFs and images, and your handwriting are
stored as files in a folder you choose (on the iPad, in iCloud Drive, or with another provider that
appears in the Files app). NotesCraft reads and writes those files on your device. Search and page
navigation run on the device.

**Camera.** If you use "Scan documents", the app asks for camera access to scan paper documents
into a notebook. The images are processed on the device with Apple's document scanner and saved only
into the notebook you are working in. Camera access is requested at that moment and can be revoked
in Settings.

**Photos.** When you insert a picture, the app uses Apple's photo picker. NotesCraft only receives
the pictures you select and does not get access to the rest of your library.

**Settings.** Preferences such as the selected pen, colours, scribble and layout options, and your
last reading position in each document are kept in the app's local settings on the device.

## Storage providers

If you keep your documents in iCloud Drive, OneDrive or another cloud provider, that provider
stores and syncs the files according to its own terms and privacy policy. NotesCraft does not
contact these services itself; the system's file coordination does the syncing.

## Sharing and export

When you export a PDF or share a document, the data goes to the place you choose in the system
share sheet (for example Mail, Messages or another app). That is always an action you start, and
NotesCraft does not send anything on its own.

## Data we collect

None. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal data, there is nothing for the
developer to store, sell, share or delete, and the App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

## Children

NotesCraft does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

As no personal data is processed by the developer, there is no data to access, correct or delete on
our side. Your files are yours: you can view, move or delete them at any time in the Files app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective
date. Changes that affect data handling will be described in the app's release notes.
