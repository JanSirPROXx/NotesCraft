NotesCraft is a notebook for iPad and Apple Pencil that keeps your notes where you want them: in ordinary files in a folder you choose, on the iPad, in iCloud Drive or with another provider in the Files app. No account, no ads, no tracking.

WRITE LIKE ON PAPER
• Fountain pen, ballpoint, brush and pencil with pressure and tilt
• Highlighter, eraser and lasso for handwriting, images and text
• Hold a stroke to turn it into a clean line, circle, rectangle, arrow or curve
• Scribble over writing to erase it
• Stickers, text boxes, images and your own reusable elements
• Paper in many styles, vertical or horizontal pages, whiteboards

CALCULATOR PEN
Write a calculation by hand, finish it with = and the answer is written next to it. It runs on your iPad and shows a ? when it is not sure instead of guessing. Teach it your own handwriting in Settings.

WORK WITH PDFs
• Annotate the original PDF with ink and text; other apps see standard annotations
• Fill in PDF forms, open password-protected PDFs, follow links and outlines
• Scan paper documents, import images, export PDFs with vector ink
• Search text boxes and PDF text, with the matches highlighted

STAY ORGANISED
• Folders, favorites, tabs for open documents, a trash with restore
• Bookmarks and page thumbnails, page templates and planners
• A free Marketplace with themes, stickers, templates and planners
• Light, dark and your own themes

YOURS
• Notes are open packages (.notecraft), PDFs and Markdown files
• Works offline; only the Marketplace uses the network
• English, Deutsch, Français, Español and Italiano: choose the language in Settings

Apple Pencil is recommended. Drawing with a finger can be switched on in Settings.
